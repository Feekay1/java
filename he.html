Comparison Opearators

Comparison opearators are used to compare two values.
They always return a boolean value (true or false)

Common Comparison Operators:

  • == equal to (value only)
  • === equal to (value and type)
  • != not equal
  • !== not equal (value and type)
  • > greater than
  • < less than



Logical Opearators

Logical opearators are used to combine or modify boolean values.
They work with true or false results (often from comparison)

Common Logical Operators:

  • && AND (all conditions must be true)
  • || OR (at least one condition must be true)
  • ! NOT (reverse the result)
